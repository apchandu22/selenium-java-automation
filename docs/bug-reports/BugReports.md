## Confirmed Defects

No confirmed application defects have been recorded in this portfolio yet. Defects will be added here only after they are reproduced and verified during an actual test execution.

## Defect Severity Guide

| Severity | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Critical | Blocks a core business flow or causes major application failure |
| High | Major functionality is unusable or significantly impacted |
| Medium | Functionality is affected but a workaround may exist |
| Low | Minor UI, content, or cosmetic issue |

## Defect Priority Guide

| Priority | Meaning |
|---|---|
| P0 | Immediate attention required |
| P1 | High business impact; should be fixed quickly |
| P2 | Normal priority |
| P3 | Low priority / can be scheduled later |

## QA Practice

A professional defect report should be:

- Reproducible
- Clear and concise
- Supported by evidence where possible
- Linked to the relevant test case
- Assigned an appropriate severity and priority
- Updated through its lifecycle
